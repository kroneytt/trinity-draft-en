# Trinity Draft: Singularity Rulebook (Quick 3-Player)

## About this rulebook

**Singularity** is an official variant that compresses Trinity Draft: a 10-card Deck and 8 Life cards, and special cards called **Holes**. A game is short, so it's a good way to introduce new players.

> *"To someone, somewhere, who is reading this letter, and who must surely be out there: find me."* The envelope held a Hole that leads to another dimension, and a special Reserve Card. Beginnings are always sudden. Hello, Singularity.

| Players | Time |
| --- | --- |
| 3 | About 45 minutes |

This book lists only what Singularity changes. Everything it doesn't mention works exactly as in the Standard rulebook, with the same terms.

Trinity Draft, its cards, names and rules are © 2025 Mikiseishindo / AsobiDays. This is a fan translation and is not affiliated with the publisher. Where this book and the official Japanese rules disagree, the official rules are correct.

## 2. What you need

- 1 set of Trinity Draft (one box).
- 3 Singularity Reserves.
- 3 α Holes.
- 3 β Holes, or the 9 different special β Holes.

The Singularity Reserves and Holes are printed at home: download them from the official site, print them, and sleeve each one together with a backing card of the same size.

## 3. 1st Stage: Draft

Make **9 packs of 6 cards**. Draft them exactly as usual (3 rounds, passing clockwise, counter-clockwise, then clockwise).

At the end of the Draft each player has **18 cards**.

## 4. 2nd Stage: Build

1. Deal each player **1 α Hole** and **2 special β Holes** at random.
2. Each player chooses 1 of their 2 special β Holes to use. Return the other one face down.
3. With your α Hole and β Hole in mind, build a **10-card Main Deck** and an **8-card Life Deck** from your 18 cards. Choose your Epic as usual.

**If anyone is new to Trinity Draft,** everyone should use the shared **β Hole** instead of special β Holes.

## 5. Final Stage: Battle

### 5.1 Setup

Set up as usual, with these changes:

- **Reserve.** Use a **Singularity Reserve** instead of the usual Reserve Card. The Singularity Reserve has no Trinity Charge.
- **Walls and Trinity Counters.** When you place the Walls, each player also gets Trinity Counters by turn order: the 1st player none, the 2nd player 1, the 3rd player 2.
- **Life Zone.** Shuffle your Life Deck and place **4 cards** at your left Wall and **4 cards** at your right Wall.
- **Holes.** Put your **2 Holes** face down in your Shadow Zone.
- **Starting Hand.** Draw **3 cards**, then add your Epic as usual.

### 5.2 Battle

- **Color Charge** gives you **2** Basic Color Cards of the matching color. For a multi-color card, both must be the same color.
- In your Main Phase you may **reveal a Hole** (chapter 6).

### 5.3 How to win

Be the first player to reach **6 Gained Life**.

In the Standard game you can't lose unless an opponent takes 4 Life from you. In Singularity it's only **2**, so be careful!

### 5.4 The Singularity Reserve

> &#91;Activate\] \<Trinity Draw> Remove 3 \[Trinity Counter\]s from your Reserve. ▶ Draw 1 card.

It holds up to 10 Trinity Counters, like the usual Reserve.

## 6. Holes

### 6.1 How Holes work

- Holes start the game face down in your **Shadow Zone**.
- In your Main Phase, pay a Hole's Cost to **reveal** it and use it.
- A revealed Hole normally stays face up in your Shadow Zone.
- Holes count as cards in the Shadow Zone, but **other cards' effects don't affect them**.
- Because your 2 Holes take up 2 of the 3 Shadow Zone slots, you can have only **1** face-down Shadow Card at a time.

### 6.2 Parts of a Hole

| Part | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Card Name | The Hole's name. |
| Cost | What you pay to reveal it. Holes have no Color Condition: any colors may be used. |
| \[On Reveal\] effect | Happens when you reveal the Hole. |
| Hole effects | Abilities you can use while the Hole is revealed. |
| Overdrive modifier | While the Hole is revealed, this number is added to your Gained Life when checking whether your \<Overdrive> abilities activate. |

**Overdrive modifier example.** You have 2 Gained Life and two revealed Holes, each with an Overdrive modifier of +3. For \<Overdrive>, you count 2 + 3 + 3 = 8, so \<Overdrive 8> activates.

### 6.3 "β Hole" Genesis

Other Holes never leave the Shadow Zone when revealed, but **"β Hole" Genesis** is a Unit, so it goes onto the Battlefield.

- It is treated like any other Unit. For example, it can be used to pay for a Shinra Summon.
- If it would be put anywhere other than a Soul, the Battlefield or the Shadow Zone, it is removed from the game instead.
- When it is summoned, a slot in your Shadow Zone opens up. You can set a new Shadow Card there as usual.
