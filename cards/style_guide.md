# Trinity Draft English Style Guide

This guide explains how the English card text is written, so every card reads the same way.

- Japanese → English terms are listed in [`master_glossary.json`](master_glossary.json).
- The card data itself is in [`blaze_database.json`](blaze_database.json) and [`phantom_database.json`](phantom_database.json).
- `python tools/validate_cards.py` checks every card against these rules.

## 1. Notation

| Form | Used for | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| `[Tag]` | timings, ability tags, actions, card-type tags, auras, colors | `[On Entry]` `[Trigger]` `[Rest]` `[Active]` `[Set] Spell` `[Quick Aura]` `[Red]` |
| `[Tag N]` / `[Tag: X]` | numbered or conditional summon rules | `[Shinra Summon 6]` `[Color Shift 2]` `[Reduced Summon: Dragon]` `[Zero Summon: Requiem 2]` |
| `<Keyword N>` | costed or conditional keyword abilities | `<Soul Flare 1>` `<Overcount 5>` `<Overdrive 8>` `<Bloody Drive 2>` `<Counter Vortex>` |
| `<Keyword>` | the same keyword mentioned without a number | `<Overdrive>` `<Overcount>` |
| `[phrase]` | a value or choice the reader works out (may contain tags) | `[the number of your Trinity Counters]` `[1 card from your Graveyard]` `[Unit or [Set] Spell]` |
| `(…)` | reminder text, and the labels (Optional), (1/Turn), (No Cost) | `(This is not destruction)` |
| `▶` | separates a condition or cost from its effect | `[Trigger] At the end of your turn. ▶ Draw 1 card.` |
| ` / ` | separates abilities in the data (one ability per line on the card) | |
| ` - ` | after a keyword's cost, and before each option in "Choose 1 of the following" lists | `<Soul Flare 1> - Send 1 Soul…` |

## 2. Vocabulary

- **Zones are capitalized:** Deck, Hand, Graveyard, Battlefield, Life Zone, Gained Life, Color Zone, Shadow Zone, Reserve, Wall, Soul. The `[EPIC Rule]` text is the one exception and stays lowercase, as printed.
- **Card types:**
  - Unit and Shadow Unit.
  - `[Shot] Spell`, `[Set] Spell` and `[Equip] Spell`.
  - Shadow `[Shot]` Spell and Shadow `[Equip]` Spell.

  The tag always comes before "Spell".
- **Traits:** Dragon, Beast, Nightmare, Elemental, Machine, War Knight, Valkyrie, Shark, Abyss, Gaia, Idea, Chaos, Void. Write them as `A/B` with no spaces, in the printed order.
- **Color Condition:** `1 Red` or `2 Blue, 2 Purple`, in the printed order, or `Colorless`. Shadow cards have none.
- **Colorless cards:** these carry the note `Colorless cards cannot be used for Color Charge.`
- **Timing labels:** `(Optional)`, `(1/Turn)`, `(2/Turn)` and `(No Cost)` come straight after the timing tag.
- **Trinity Counters:**
  - Gaining them is written `+N [Trinity Counter]`, optionally followed by `(Add N Trinity Counter(s) to your Reserve)`.
  - Counting them is written `N or more [Trinity Counter]s`.
- **Burst:** starts with `[Burst]`. Epic cards have `[EPIC Rule]` in its place.
- **Betrayal:** `[Burst] Betrayal - If it is during a Burst Check, you take 1 damage.` This appears on all Void cards.
- **Phases:** Draw Phase, Active Phase, Main Phase 1, Combat Phase, Main Phase 2, End Phase.

## 3. Wording patterns

- **Choosing a target:** `Choose 1 Unit on the Battlefield and destroy it.` / `Choose 1 of your Units.`
- **Optional costs:** `[On Entry] (Optional) Discard 1 card from your Hand. ▶ Draw 2 cards.`
- **Conditions end with a period before ▶:** `[Trigger] When your Unit is destroyed. ▶ …`
  - On the card, each ▶ starts a new line.
  - The phrase `use this card's effect from ▶.` refers back to the arrow and stays on the same line.
- **Battles outside an attack:** `have them battle. (This battle is not an attack)`
- **Sending a card away without destroying it:** `send this card to the Graveyard. (This is not destruction)`
- **Exact values:** `exactly "1" Cost`, `its Cost becomes "X"`.
- **Plurals follow the number:** 1 card / 2 cards, 1 Soul / 2 Souls.
- **Spell reminders:** every Spell starts with the reminder sentence printed for its type.
  - `[Shot] Spell`: `After resolving its effect, send this card to its owner's Graveyard.`
  - `[Set] Spell`: `After use, it remains on the Battlefield and exerts its effects.`
  - `[Equip] Spell`: `Pay the Cost to put this onto the Battlefield. Then, pay the [Equip Cost] to equip it to a Unit. When the equipped Unit leaves the Battlefield, the Equipment remains on the Battlefield.`

## 4. Keyword wording

Each keyword is always written the same way. N is the number printed on the card, X is the condition, and … is card-specific text.
